# Trancy

*A simple guide to learning languages with videos*

## 1. What is Trancy?

**Trancy** is a **browser extension** designed to help you learn languages—especially **English**—by watching videos.

It works mainly with:

-   **YouTube**
-   **Netflix** (paid plans)
-   Other subtitle-supported video platforms

Trancy adds powerful learning features **on top of videos**, such as:

-   Dual subtitles
-   Word lookup
-   Sentence translation
-   Vocabulary collection

You learn naturally while watching content you enjoy.

## 2. Who is Trancy for?

Trancy is suitable for:

-   Beginners learning English
-   Students preparing for exams (IELTS, TOEFL, CET)
-   People improving listening and vocabulary
-   Anyone who learns better through videos

No technical background is required.

## 3. How Trancy Works (Core Idea)

The idea is simple:

>   **Watch → Read subtitles → Click words → Understand → Remember**

Instead of passively watching videos, Trancy turns them into **interactive learning material**.

## 4. Installing Trancy

### Step 1: Choose a Browser

Trancy works best on:

-   **Chrome**
-   **Edge**
-   **Brave**

### Step 2: Install the Extension

1.  Open your browser’s extension store
2.  Search for **“Trancy”**
3.  Click **Add to browser**
4.  Pin Trancy to the toolbar (recommended)

That’s it—no complex setup.

## 5. First Time Setup

After installation:

1.  Click the **Trancy icon**
2.  Select your **learning language** (e.g. English)
3.  Select your **native language** (e.g. Chinese)

Trancy will now translate subtitles into your native language.

## 6. Using Trancy with YouTube (Most Common)

### Step 1: Open a YouTube Video

Choose a video that:

-   Has **English subtitles**
-   Is not too fast (talk shows, vlogs, interviews are great)

### Step 2: Enable Trancy

-   Trancy activates automatically
-   You’ll see **dual subtitles** appear

Example:

```
English subtitle
Chinese translation
```

## 7. Key Features Explained

### 1️⃣ Dual Subtitles

-   Shows **English + translation**
-   Helps you understand meaning instantly
-   Reduces the need to pause the video

Best for beginners.

### 2️⃣ Click-to-Translate Words

-   Click **any word** in the subtitle
-   See:
    -   Meaning
    -   Pronunciation
    -   Example usage

This is much faster than using a dictionary.

### 3️⃣ Sentence Translation

-   Hover over a sentence
-   See the full translated meaning
-   Useful for complex grammar or long sentences

### 4️⃣ Replay Sentence / Loop

-   Replay a single sentence
-   Improve listening and pronunciation
-   Very useful for shadowing practice

### 5️⃣ Vocabulary Collection

-   Save words you don’t know
-   Build your personal word list
-   Review later

Think of it as **video-based Anki**, but simpler.

## 8. Recommended Learning Workflow (Beginner)

Here’s a simple routine:

1.  Play a video you like
2.  Watch with **dual subtitles**
3.  Click unfamiliar words
4.  Save important words
5.  Replay difficult sentences
6.  Don’t try to understand *everything*

**15–30 minutes per day is enough.**

## 9. Tips for Beginners

-   ✅ Choose **slow, clear speakers**
-   ✅ Watch content you already like
-   ❌ Don’t pause every sentence
-   ❌ Don’t memorize everything at once
-   ✅ Focus on **high-frequency words**

Consistency is more important than speed.

## 10. Free vs Paid

-   **Paid version**:
    -   Netflix support
    -   More advanced features
    -   Better for long-term learners

You can start free and upgrade later.
